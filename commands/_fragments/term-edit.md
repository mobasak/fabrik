## ⚠️ Termination contract — READ FIRST (the rule agents skip)

This is a LOOP, not a one-shot. It ends — and you may {{DONE_ACT}} — **only when the CLOSING round CONFIRMED zero defects, confirmed by EXECUTION (a pinned read quoted beside the sentence, a count re-derived from its primary source, a `path:line` resolved at the pinned SHA, a gate line run, a URL status probed by the orchestrator), with nothing left unexecuted (`confirmed: 0, fixed: 0, unexecuted: 0`) and the md5 unchanged (D-206, D-212)**. THE CLOSING ROUND, wherever this contract names it: a delta round that carried a fresh non-authoring seat — or, for a solo self-convergence loop (`62-using-subagents.md` § Role separation's one sanctioned exception: a command whose loop dispatches NO seat that reads the {{ARTIFACT}} for it — no finder, grounder, reconciler or auditor, `62`'s seat classes — anywhere; its example is `/fabrik-ui-design`), a second full fresh read of its own by the re-derivation method, its independence deferred to the paired review command that follows it. Fixes open new gaps, so **the round in which you edited the {{ARTIFACT}} is NEVER the last round** — it MUST be followed by another round. **The pass SHAPE:** round 1 is the ONLY full pass of a seat-carrying loop, and a solo loop's closing read is its second — round 1 reads the WHOLE {{ARTIFACT}}, cut into DISJOINT slices: by SECTION for the spec and plan reviews (Opus on the rule/grammar sections, Sonnet on the rest — one seat each for a small artifact, more slices when `dispatch_headroom.py --slices opus=N,sonnet=N` sizes them; ledger must SAY so.** RE-DERIVE every factual claim inside the delta — every count, enumeration, and line anchor —

Convergence under one method is method-stability, not truth. **Minimum two rounds, ALWAYS** — even a quiet round 1 must be confirmed by a closing round (`command_run.py` prints TERMINAL only after ≥2 rounds, D-211); accuracy outranks round-count.

Anti-cheat (mechanical, not vibes): record the `md5sum` of the WORKING-TREE {{ARTIFACT}} at the pin of every round (`md5(start)`) and after its fixes (`md5(end)`) — never hash the pin itself, which is frozen by construction; residue fixes are the previous round's edits and precede its `md5(end)`, so the closing round's `md5(start)` equals that hash.
