**Large reads go to seats (D-363, § 4.9 finding 4).** A read the lead would make of more than ~30 k tokens
(~120 KB — a long spec or plan, the ACTIVE rules packs together, `agents-fabrik.md`, a fabrik-lib module tree, a
prior transcript) goes to ONE seat per source — `Explore` for the repo, `fabrik-researcher` for the web —
briefed with the QUESTION the read must answer, never "summarise this", and returning at most ~2 k tokens with
every claim anchored `path:line`. A Haiku seat pays from ~10 k tokens. The lead re-reads only the anchored lines
it will cite, and EXECUTES every claim it writes into the artifact: a summary is navigation, never evidence.
Cobra: the cheapest way to satisfy this is a summary with no anchors — a claim without its `path:line` is
re-read by the lead, never cited.
