⚠️ **PIN the subject before you dispatch, or the pass is not reproducible.** A read-only finder cannot pin what it reads, and the dispatching session keeps editing — the two obligations are in tension by construction ("converge in THIS invocation" keeps the author editing; "the author's own re-read never counts" requires a concurrent independent reader). Measured twice in two days: a finder watched its subject's md5 change mid-review and had to reclassify four findings as *superseded by the edit* (`01M1KN0H6JRPXVFKH911425K2Y`), and an author-blind reviewer watched a spine go 320 → 514 → 552 lines and a ticket split underneath it — briefed on 8 tickets, it graded 9 (`01M1KYMG27FMEQXA89A2A4B2ZD`). Both survived only because the finder SAID so. So: **`cp` the artifact to a stable scratch path at dispatch (`<scratchpad>/<artifact>.pass<N>.md`) and brief finders on THAT path**, carry its **md5 in the brief**, and require the finder to **state the hash it actually read**. A hash mismatch is a re-dispatch, not a judgement call; a finding against an unpinned subject is dismissible as stale, which is the same as not having run the pass. Record the snapshot md5 in the pass-ledger row.
