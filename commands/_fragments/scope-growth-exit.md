> **The exit is the verdict the round you just recorded PRINTED — read it off, do not re-derive it.**
> `command_run.py` computes the scope-growth bar at every `round` and prints one of three things.
> **`⚠️ SCOPE GROWTH`** — the loop is correcting its own corrections: stop HUNTING, name and fix every
> confirmed defect still open anywhere in the window, route genuinely own-fix residue to a backlog row
> with a named destination, and close on the ORIGINAL delta's state. **`↗ ESCALATE`** — the ratio was
> computed and NOT met, so these are the artifact's own defects and the surface outgrew this pass:
> route up per step 5. **`? SCOPE GROWTH UNCOMPUTABLE`** — a window round never stated a readable
> `--confirmed`/`--own-fix` pair and it would decide the verdict: state it and re-record before
> deciding. ⚠️ **If it printed nothing at all, the stop was never computed — that is NOT a verdict and
> must never be read as "this is not the scope-growth case".** Maintained once, at
> `/opt/fabrik/commands/_fragments/scope-growth-exit.md`; every copy in this command is a render of it.
